Remembering I Have a Bad Sense of Humor

*Channels my 14 year old self with her delusions of grandeur and main character syndrome*

So hey guys, as you might know, I had a blog. It had all of ... 4? Subscribers. One of which was probably my dad.

I had the pleasure of revisiting my blog and reading some of the... *shudders* posts. I cringed the way you do at your unfinished self. But then I looked past the words I'd written. I'd forgotten how... earnest I was?

I was so direct. I had the audacity to think, I have thoughts. The internet exists. These two facts clearly belong together.
